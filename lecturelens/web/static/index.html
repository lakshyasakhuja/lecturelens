Three ways to remember a lecture.

You might recall a phrase, a word on a slide, or just what the diagram looked like. LectureLens searches all three at once.

  • What was said.

    Every word is transcribed with its own timestamp, so a result lands on the sentence, not just the lecture.

    Whisper large-v3-turbo

  • What was on the slide.

    Slide text is read from the frame where the slide is complete, after every bullet has appeared.

    PP-OCRv5

  • What it looked like.

    Each slide is also embedded as a picture. Describe the plot or the diagram, and it finds the slide.

    SigLIP 2

  • Fused by rank, not by score.

    Keyword scores, embedding similarities and image similarities live on different scales. Reciprocal Rank Fusion ignores the scores and uses only each ranker's order, so there is nothing to calibrate.

    RRF(d) = Σ 160 + rank(d)

    The heavy models run once, when a lecture is indexed. A search needs two small text encoders and a few matrix products.

Index once. Search anywhere.

Indexing is the expensive part, so it happens once, offline, on a GPU. What is left to do online is small enough for a single CPU.

  • A moment is a slide.

    The unit of search is one slide and the words spoken while it was on screen. Slides that stay up for minutes are cut into overlapping windows.

  • Added is not replaced.

    A bullet appearing only adds ink. A new slide removes ink and draws more. The slide detector reads the sign of the change to tell them apart.

  • No vector database.

    A semester is a few thousand moments. An exhaustive dot product over that is faster than a network round trip to a database would be.

The library.

The lectures this demo searches.

    Measured, not claimed.

    Each signal is switched on and off against hand-written questions, so the numbers show which signal helps for which kind of question.

    Benchmark in progress.

    The benchmark is 60 questions written by hand before retrieval was tuned, in four kinds: keyword, paraphrase, spoken-only and visual. Twenty are for tuning; forty are reported once. Results will appear here when they have been measured. Until then, this page shows no numbers.
